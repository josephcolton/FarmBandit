Farmer walk sheet (64x64 frames, 9 per row; rows are north, west, south, east)

Built for Farm Bandit by layering pieces from the Universal LPC Spritesheet
Character Generator (https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator).
